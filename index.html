    Case study · Cybersecurity86 seconds · Bangla · 9:16

    Your keyboard's sound can give away your password

    The challenge
    Explain an acoustic side-channel attack (a 2023 Durham, Surrey and Royal Holloway study that recovered 95% of keystrokes from typing sounds) to a general audience, in under 90 seconds, without losing accuracy.
    The approach
    Open on a situation the viewer has lived (typing a password on a Zoom call), then show the mechanism in four visual steps: every key sounds different → each click becomes a spectrogram image → an image-recognition AI learns to read them → letters rebuild the password. End on the catch and three practical defences.
    What was made
    Research from the original paper, script, custom drawn laptop, waveform and spectrogram graphics, a count-up and unit charts, word-synced captions, sound design and edit.

    Process

    From “it's complicated” to final cut.

    You approve every stage before we move on, so there are no surprises at the end.

    1. Discover

      A short call and your materials. We learn the product, the audience and the one thing viewers must remember.

      You get: a one-page brief

    2. Research & script

      We dig in until we can explain it simply, then write a tight, accurate script.

      You get: script for approval

    3. Storyboard & style

      Key frames that show how each line will look, in your brand's colours.

      You get: storyboard & style frames

    4. Animate

      Voiceover, animation, sound design and music, built around the approved board.

      You get: full draft to review

    5. Deliver

      Final master plus the cuts you need: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, captions and short teasers.

      You get: ready-to-post files

    About the studio

    A studio named after a magnifying glass.

    আতসকাচ (AtoshKach) means magnifying glass in Bangla — the tool that brings small, blurry things into focus. That's the job.

    The studio grew out of the আতসকাচ channel, where our founder explains technology, AI, cybersecurity and money through original animation, handling every step from research and scripting to design, animation, sound and edit. Behind it is a production system with its own brand book, reusable motion components and a review loop driven by audience retention data, so videos come out consistent and fast. Now we bring the same approach to companies that need their own complex story told clearly.

    Small by design: you talk directly to the person making your video.

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Your portfolio is in Bangla. Can you make English videos?

    Yes. The channel is in Bangla because that's its audience, but the visual storytelling is the same in any language. Client videos are produced with native-quality English voiceover, Bangla, or both.

    How long does a project take?

    A 60–90 second explainer usually takes 1–3 weeks from kickoff, depending mostly on how quickly each stage is approved. Short social pieces can be faster. Rush delivery is available for an extra fee.

    Is voiceover included?

    Every video is built around a voiceover. You can use your own recording, a high-quality AI voice in English or Bangla (included), or a hired voice artist, which is quoted separately. Videos can also work without voice, using on-screen text and sound.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on length, style and how many versions you need. Every project gets a fixed quote after a short call, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

    How many revisions do we get?

    Two rounds at each stage: script, storyboard and animation. Send all feedback for a round in one document. A new concept, a rewritten script or a change of direction after approval is quoted as new work, which is why you approve each stage before the next begins.

    Do you work with agencies under NDA?

    Yes. White-label work is credited to you, we sign your NDA, and we never contact or market to your clients.

    How do payments work for international clients?

    50% to start and 50% before the final, unwatermarked files are delivered. International clients can pay by Wise, Payoneer or bank transfer; clients in Bangladesh by bank transfer or bKash. Commercial usage rights are included; editable project files are available for an extra fee.
